Where the conclusions stop

These limits are part of understanding the project.

Funded, resolved loans only

Rejected applicants and unresolved loans are absent. Later cohorts may overrepresent quickly resolved loans.

No historical label timestamps

Resolution dates are unavailable. This is a later-cohort comparison, not a simulation of exactly what a lender knew on each date.

Scores are not calibrated probabilities

The model understates observed outcome rates. No one-year PD, profit or real approval-policy claim is supported.

A limited research sample

Historical US marketplace loans, no uncertainty intervals or fairness evaluation. Transfer to Austrian banking has not been tested.
